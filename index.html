The Masterclass

Where This Goes Deep

Four one-on-one areas of focus, built from a career spent bowling leg-spin at the highest level and two decades of reading the game as a broadcaster.

Leg-spin & craft

Grip, wrist position, release and disguise — the fundamentals that separate a leg-break from a wrong'un, taught the way a working international spinner learned to bowl them.

Flight, drift and variation

Using loop and pace changes to draw batters forward — reading a pitch and a batter's footwork to decide which ball to bowl, and when.

Match awareness & field craft

Setting fields, building pressure and thinking a spell ahead — the tactical side of bowling built over years of first-class and international cricket.

One-on-one mentoring

Personal coaching sessions built around the individual bowler — technique, temperament and the mental discipline that wrist-spin demands.

About

Laxman Sivaramakrishnan

The Spin Wizard is now turning decades of experience into the next generation of cricketing excellence.

Born on 31 December 1965 in Madras, Laxman Sivaramakrishnan announced himself to Indian cricket as a teenager. A right-arm leg-break bowler, he took seven wickets on his Ranji Trophy debut for Tamil Nadu against Delhi in 1981–82, aged just sixteen. Within a year, he had toured Pakistan with the Indian team, and in 1983, at seventeen, made his Test debut against the West Indies.

His breakthrough season came in 1984–85, when a series of outstanding performances against England, including a ten-wicket haul, established him as one of India's most exciting wrist-spinners. He was named Indian Cricketer of the Year and was part of the Indian squad that won the World Championship of Cricket in Australia.

Today, the Spin Wizard brings that same knowledge, experience and passion to coaching. Through LS Masterclass, he works with aspiring cricketers to develop the craft of spin bowling, understand the nuances of the game and build the mindset required to perform at the highest level.

From mastering the art of leg-spin to understanding match situations, technique and the finer details that separate good bowlers from great ones, LS Masterclass is about passing on lessons earned through a lifetime in cricket.

His Cricket Journey

A Career Told in Moments

  1. 1981–82

    Ranji Trophy debut

    Took 7 wickets on his first-class debut for Tamil Nadu against Delhi, aged sixteen — an opening statement that put his name on the national radar.

  2. 1982–83

    Tour of Pakistan

    Selected for India's tour of Pakistan as a teenager, a first taste of international cricket at the highest level.

  3. 1983

    Test debut vs West Indies

    Made his Test debut in Antigua on 28 April 1983 at the age of seventeen, becoming one of the youngest Test cricketers in Indian history.

  4. 1984–85

    Career-best spells against England

    Recorded best figures of 6 for 64 and a ten-wicket match haul, the peak of a nine-Test career that yielded 26 wickets with three five-wicket innings.

  5. 1985

    Cricketer of the Year & World Championship

    Named Indian Cricket Cricketer of the Year and part of the squad that won the World Championship of Cricket in Australia.

  6. 1987–98

    Domestic cricket & final ODI

    Continued a long first-class career with Tamil Nadu and later Baroda, playing his last ODI for India in 1987 and retiring from first-class cricket in 1998.

  7. 2000–Present

    Broadcaster & cricket administrator

    Began a long commentary career in 2000 and has served as a players' representative on the ICC Cricket Committee.
